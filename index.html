About

Bio

Yoanna Turura is a software engineer at Amazon Web Services in Jersey City, where she builds authentication, authorization, and access-control infrastructure for sensitive financial workflows, along with tools that surface insights into operating expenses. She graduated from MIT in 2025 with a bachelor's in Artificial Intelligence and Decision Making.

At MIT, she worked as a machine learning researcher on the ML4H team in the Uhler Lab at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard. There she led the development of Latentverse, an open-source toolkit for evaluating learned representations, and presented it as first author at CHIL 2025.

The work she finds most meaningful sits where models meet people: improving how a model learns while staying close to the real application and the people using it. Teaching is part of that for her. She has designed and taught introductory programming courses for hundreds of first-time programmers, including in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

She writes for fun on Substack, no AI involved.

Research

Interests

Her current research centers on federated learning: training a shared model across many devices or institutions without pooling their raw data in one place.

∇

Federated optimization

Making decentralized training converge reliably and efficiently when data is scattered, uneven, and private.

◇

Low-resource deployment

Adapting federated learning for settings with limited compute, bandwidth, and intermittent connectivity.

≈

Fairness across clients

Ensuring a shared model works well for every participant, not just the largest or best-resourced ones.

The broader thread: Yoanna is interested in AI as public interest technology: research that has a direct, positive effect on people, and systems that change how AI gets deployed into communities that have historically been left out.

Past work: evaluating self-supervised representations and their stability under distribution shift in multimodal clinical data (ECG, MRI), and adapting multilingual language models to code-switched text.
